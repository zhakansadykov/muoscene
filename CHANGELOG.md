# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Repository skeleton: package layout by layers, configuration of tests, linting and private-data guard.
- Draft conventions (`docs/conventions.md`) and detailed roadmap for 0.1 (`docs/roadmap.md`).
- Design review of the skeleton: `TrackSet` moved from `io` to `detectors.tracks` (layer 2), because `synth` and `data` (layer 4) could not import it from layer 5; `pipeline` module for orchestration shared by the CLI and Python users.
- Import rules tightened: relative imports are resolved by the layer test; optional dependencies must be imported lazily; the computing core must not import graphics; module docstrings must state their layer.
- Private-data guard: scans whole files for the marker, refuses survey and CAD formats outside `examples/` and `tests/`, runs in CI on all tracked files, covered by tests.
- Metadata: `.zenodo.json` checked against `CITATION.cff` by a test, a pre-commit hook and CI; licence, authors and repository URL checked across `pyproject.toml` and `CITATION.cff`.
- Tooling: ruff pinned to one version for pre-commit, CI and development; local hooks run in their own environment; CI runs the format check, keeps all matrix jobs (`fail-fast: false`) and cancels superseded runs.
- Mulder and Calzone spike (roadmap 0.1-02): results in `docs/notes/mulder.md`, reproducible by `docs/notes/spike/mulder_spike.py`.
- Architecture decision: hybrid on top of Mulder and Calzone. They become core dependencies (`mulder>=0.3.7,<0.4`, `calzone>=1.2,<2`); numba is dropped; the `mc` extra is removed. Stub modules renamed to the new design: `geometry.builder`, `geometry.pathtable`, `transport.table`, `transport.direct`, `flux.atmosphere`. A test confines backend imports to the wrapping modules.
- Conventions revised: kinetic energy at the detector, detector energy response, altitude cut-off, flux tables, equivalent opacity, bodies and anomalies. Roadmap for 0.1 rewritten.
- Conventions approved (15 November 2026): kinetic energy, default detector threshold 1 GeV, reference flux at the site altitude in 0.1, domain of validity with refusal outside it. Roadmap: task 0.1-05b, reference at the site altitude.
- Reference numbers (roadmap 0.1-03): the preliminary calculation ported to `tests/regression/legacy.py`, bit-identical to the original scripts, and frozen on a synthetic canyon (`tests/regression/data/legacy_reference.json`); the Mulder and Calzone numbers of the spike frozen in `tests/regression/data/mulder_reference.json` and tested at a relative tolerance of 1e-5 (platform spread up to 3.1e-6, on macOS), so that a release of Mulder or Calzone that changes the physics fails; `docs/legacy.md`.
- Spike note corrected: integrals over all energies above sea level now run from the altitude cut-off in ln(K - K_cut); season, geomagnetic and E8 numbers changed by up to 1.3 %; E8 added to the spike script.
- Materials (roadmap 0.1-04): `muoscene.materials` with compositions, mixtures, soils by dry density and water content, Mulder registration under names derived from the definition, CSDA ranges, weights and equivalent opacity; a library of water, standard rock, concrete, air, quartz, calcite and two soil skeletons with sources; tests against the PDG range tables and the spike. `docs/notes/materials.md`: sizes of the choices (mean excitation energy, density effect in porous soils, water content, skeleton chemistry).
- Conventions refined: equivalent-opacity weights depend on energy, w(K) = R_ref(K) / R_m(K); a soil is a mixture of skeleton and water with bulk density ρ_d (1 + w); every material carries an explicit mean excitation energy.
- Review of 16 November 2026, an independent check of numbers, tests and documents. `docs/numbers.md`: the headline numbers with their conditions. Every significance states its detector threshold; the reference void above 0.2 and 1 GeV and a slab thinner than the air below the site are frozen (`mulder_reference.json`: 63 cases, the 57 earlier ones unchanged). The scattering share of the reference void is a mean over three seeds, 0.74 ± 0.02 without a threshold and 0.92 ± 0.02 above 1 GeV: "about 9.2σ" becomes 8.4–8.9σ; `tests/validation/test_scattering.py` runs the discrete mode. Spike note and `docs/legacy.md` corrected (speed ratio, cut-off at 45°, nearest-node error, decay).
- Materials: a repeated element in a composition and energies or opacities outside the tables are refused instead of being dropped or extrapolated.
- Private-data guard: outside `examples/` and `tests/` only a short list of file types is allowed, in place of a list of refused survey formats.
- CI: Mulder and Calzone pinned for the tests (`.github/constraints.txt`); a weekly `upstream` workflow runs the newest releases and the validation tests; Python 3.13 added; numpy ≥ 2.0.
- Spike script: the pier is an invented one; `--seed` and `--threshold` for the scattering experiment; E7, E10 and E12 and the figure recomputed.
- Conventions, revision 3: Mulder applies decay along the air path (the text said it did not); numbers are quoted for detector thresholds of 0.2 and 1 GeV until the response of the emulsion is known; the site-altitude reference is the default from 0.1.1; the heading of the expected-map section restored.
- Roadmap: tasks 0.1-05b (reference at the site altitude, now from PARMA with MCEq as a cross-check and with the soft component) and 0.1-12 (3D scene) move behind the release of 0.1.0; new task 0.1-09b, the preliminary scheme with the new physics.
- Conventions, revision 4: a flux table holds the open-sky flux at the detector altitude on a grid of kinetic energy and sine of elevation and takes the opacity through the CSDA range and stopping power of its reference material, in place of a third grid dimension computed under a dense slab; the slab stays as the check of every new table. The energy response of an integral is always given explicitly.
- Flux tables (roadmap 0.1-05): `muoscene.flux` with `FluxTable`, `EnergyResponse`, `MulderTable` (atmosphere model, optional geomagnetic field of a date, altitude cut-off found, stored and enforced, comparison with Mulder under a dense slab at every build, disk cache in the user cache directory by a hash of the inputs) and `LegacyTable` (the preliminary calculation, bit for bit). Within 0.045 % of Mulder under a slab up to 1 TeV, integrals above 0.2 and 1 GeV within 6e-5; requests below the cut-off, beyond 3e4 g/cm² or outside the grid are refused. `docs/notes/flux.md`; new rows in `docs/numbers.md`.
- Frozen numbers: seven cases added to `tests/regression/data/mulder_reference.json` (70; the 63 earlier ones bit-identical): open sky at a site 430 m below sea level, and the flux above 0.2 and 1 GeV under 0.5, 2 and 20 m of soil at 1150 m.
- Roadmap: at 1150 m a detector threshold of 1 GeV holds in the open sky from 15.7° of elevation, not from 15°; the altitude at which the table of a detector under soil is taken is left to task 0.1-08.
- Geometry (roadmap 0.1-06): `muoscene.geometry` with `Ground` (a surface profile extruded along y, horizontal `Layer`s), `Structure` (boxes, vertical cylinders, `pile_grid`), `Cut` and `Anomaly`; a builder to a Calzone tree (pieces of ground as meshes side by side, cuts subtracted, structures split at layer tops, anomalies as daughters of their host); `Scene.scan` and `PathTable`, with the opacity under a density hypothesis from one scan; analytic checks of a scene and the overlap check of Calzone as `Scene.check`. The air of a scene has the density of the atmosphere model at the site altitude in a box 1 m larger than the bodies. A scan refuses what Mulder answers with a crash, an endless loop or a wrong number: a point outside the scene, a nan or a direction that is not a unit vector, a point on a boundary, a line of sight cut by the limits of the model; a void is given to Mulder as 1e-9 kg/m³, not as zero. Frozen wall paths reproduced within 1.1e-13 m, analytic chords within 3e-12 m, 2.7e5 to 4.0e5 directions/s on the invented pier on a Linux VM and 5.4e4 to 5.9e4 on macOS on the same machine; the speed test is marked `validation`. `docs/notes/geometry.md`; a geometry section in `docs/numbers.md`.
- Frozen numbers: six cases added to `tests/regression/data/mulder_reference.json` (76; the 70 earlier ones bit-identical): the reference void and the open sky above 1 GeV against the height of the air of a local geometry above the brow and its density. `mulder_cases.build_scene` and `reference_void` take that height and density; their defaults are those of the earlier cases.
- Roadmap: the azimuth of the axes of a scene goes to task 0.1-07; a scene of 0.1-06 has no rotations.
- Flux note: the command that runs one test file is `python -m pytest`; a bare `pytest` on a file of `tests/unit` does not find `tests.regression`.
- Site and detectors I (roadmap 0.1-07): `muoscene.site` with `Site` — the origin of the world frame and the azimuth of the axes of a scene; `Site.scan` is the one place where points and directions pass between ENU and those axes. `muoscene.detectors` with `Pose` (axes and slopes of the conventions, round trips to round-off), `DirectionGrid` (rectangular bins in the slopes with exact solid angles, also for the part of a bin within the acceptance circle, by a contour integral; sub-samples whose weights add up to it; a default grid of 40 × 40 bins of 0.05 with 4 × 4 sub-samples), `LinesOfSight`, the `Detector` interface (accepted circle, energy response, efficiency, sensitive area against the slopes) and `EmulsionStack` with `track_density`, which sums the flux over both sides of a film and states its threshold, exposure and field of view. A detector takes a flux table and the opacity along its lines of sight and calls the table with its own response; open sky below the cut-off of the sea-level reference is refused, not returned as zero. Met: bin solid angles add up to the cone within 2e-15; the frozen track densities of the preliminary calculation within 0.03 % (horizontal films) and 0.10 % (tilted) at a Monte Carlo error of 0.21 to 0.39 %, and to 4e-16 on its own directions; Mulder through the scene within 0.06 %. `docs/notes/detectors.md` with the decisions of 21 November 2026; a section in `docs/numbers.md`.
- Geometry: `Scene` takes `azimuth_deg`, the azimuth of its +y axis from true north, and passes it to the local frame of Mulder, which needs it for the geomagnetic field (0.25 % between east and west above 1 GeV); no scan changes, and the default, 0, is the behaviour of before. `Scene` keeps `latitude_deg`, `longitude_deg` and `azimuth_deg` as attributes.
- Frozen numbers: two cases added to `tests/regression/data/mulder_reference.json` (78; the 76 earlier ones bit-identical): the density of muon tracks on a horizontal film in the niches 12 and 22 m below a flat brow at 1150 m, above 0.2 and 1 GeV on the side of the ground and above 1 GeV on the side of the canyon.
- Forward model (roadmap 0.1-08): `muoscene.model` with `expected_map`, which gives an `ExpectedMap` — an `xarray.Dataset` over detector, hypothesis and the bins of a `DirectionGrid` with the variables of the conventions — for `Station`s (a detector, its pose, the flux table of its altitude, its exposure) and a `HypothesisSet` (no anomaly, a void, a decompaction, other bulk densities: sums over one scan). `muoscene.transport` with the equivalent opacity of a path through several materials, weights between the first energy of the detector response and the energy needed above the path, found by iteration, less the air the solid bodies displace; with `flux_domain`, which tells where a table has a flux; and with the direct continuous flux of Mulder through the scene, against which every run checks the fast path on 256 random lines of sight per detector and refuses above 0.5 %. Lines of sight a table has no flux for are refused or, on request, marked bin by bin with the reason (`status`), never filled with zeros. Met on the invented pier at 1150 m: rms 0.014 % and at most 0.033 % above 1 GeV, 0.061 % above 0.2 GeV; a uniform slab within 0.021 % of Mulder; a hypothesis without a new scan equal to a rebuilt scene to 1e-12. `docs/notes/forward.md`; a section in `docs/numbers.md`.
- Detectors: `Detector.counts`, tracks per bin from a flux table and the opacity along the accepted lines of sight. Site: `Site.differential_flux`, the flux of Mulder through a scene along lines given in ENU.
- Frozen numbers: two cases added to `tests/regression/data/mulder_reference.json` (80; the 78 earlier ones bit-identical): the flux above 0.2 and 1 GeV through the invented pier of the spike, with and without its void, along 77 lines of sight.
- CI: Windows joins the test matrix, with LF line endings kept at checkout; muoscene had not been run on Windows before.
- Roadmap revised (21 November 2026): it now says who the program is for — the users of a group that exposes and scans detectors, on Windows, macOS and Linux, most of whom do not write code — and what each version lets such a user do. In 0.1 the examples, the command and the figures with a report (0.1-12, moved into 0.1.0) come before significance, installation is a task of its own (0.1-14) and the 3D scene is 0.1-12b; 0.2 gets detectors as a table of coordinates and directions, 0.3 a workbench (a local application on the same core), 0.4 a reader for the output of the processing program of the scanning group and data against model in the workbench, 1.0 a packaged application and a guide in English and Russian. No task is spent on an effect below 0.2 % until scattering and a measured energy response are in the maps.
- Windows: importing muoscene gives the process a `HOME`, the home directory of the user, when it has none. Mulder 0.3.7 finds its cache of physics tables through `$HOME` alone, and on the first run of the tests on Windows 144 of them failed with "bad cache (could not resolve $HOME)"; a user on Windows would have met the same. The cache of Mulder is `.cache/mulder` under the home directory on every system.
