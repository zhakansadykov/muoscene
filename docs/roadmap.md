# Roadmap

| Version | Target | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | early Jan 2027 | scenes of primitives through Calzone and Mulder, flux tables with the sea-level reference above its cut-off, parity with the preliminary calculation on a synthetic canyon |
| 0.1.1 | after 0.1.0 | open-sky reference at the site altitude with the soft component; first 3D scene |
| 0.2 | May 2027 | real sites: coordinate systems, meshes, survey import, layer horizons, voxels |
| 0.3 | Aug 2027 | physics and planning: scattering, mixed-mode tables for thick overburden, emulsion fading and track-density budget, systematics, profile likelihood, sensitivity volume, scheme optimisation |
| 0.4 | Nov 2027 | data: synthetic tracks, track readers, comparison and fits; straw telescope |
| 1.0 | Dec 2027 – Mar 2028 | validation suite, documentation, Zenodo DOI, PyPI, software paper |
| 2.0 | 2029 | inversion on the voxel grid; real exposure data |

One task is one working session and one or a few commits. A task is done when it has code, tests, docstrings, a `CHANGELOG.md` line and its box ticked here.

Architecture (decided 13 November 2026, `docs/notes/mulder.md`): geometry navigation and muon physics come from Calzone and Mulder; muoscene adds the scene description, a fast flux path through tables built by Mulder, detector responses, planning, and comparison with data.

## 0.1 — scenes, flux tables, parity

Goal: `muoscene plan examples/canyon_demo/run.yaml` computes expected maps and significance for a synthetic canyon with a pier at the site altitude and, in legacy mode, reproduces the preliminary sensitivity calculation. The reference at the site altitude and the rendered scene follow in 0.1.1. Conventions — `docs/conventions.md`.

- [x] **0.1-02 Prior-art spike: Mulder and Calzone.** Thirteen experiments and the decision: hybrid on top of Mulder and Calzone. `docs/notes/mulder.md`, `docs/notes/spike/mulder_spike.py`.
- [x] **0.1-01 Conventions.** Approved on 15 November 2026: kinetic energy at the detector, default threshold 1 GeV, reference at the site altitude in 0.1, runs outside the domain of validity refused.
- [x] **0.1-03 Reference numbers.** The preliminary scripts ported to `tests/regression/legacy.py` (bit-identical to the originals) and frozen on a synthetic canyon in `legacy_reference.json` (55 cases, 10.31σ for the reference void); the spike frozen in `mulder_reference.json` (57 cases at a relative tolerance of 10⁻⁵, above the spread between Linux and macOS, including the tables behind discrete transport and the wall paths of the synthetic canyon); `docs/legacy.md` with the sizes of the simplifications. Found on the way: integrals over all energies across the altitude cut-off were wrong by up to 1.3 % in the spike (season and geomagnetic numbers corrected in `docs/notes/mulder.md`); Mulder gives the air of a local geometry sea-level density: 1000 m of it above the brow lowers the counts of the reference void at 1150 m by 1 % (0.1-06: air at the site density, or a thin air layer).
- [x] **0.1-04 Materials.** `muoscene.materials`: materials from formulas, element fractions or oxide analyses with an explicit mean excitation energy (tabulated, or the Berger–Seltzer rule); mixtures; soils from dry density and gravimetric water content as a mixture of skeleton and water, so that Mulder takes the density effect at the particle density; names in Mulder derived from the definition; CSDA ranges, energy to cross, weights w(K) and equivalent opacity. Library with sources: water, standard rock, concrete, air, quartz, calcite, silicate and loess skeletons. Mulder ranges agree with the PDG tables within 0.52 %; the spike thresholds are reproduced exactly; concrete weighs 0.9952 against the spike loam at 7 GeV; moisture round trip to 1e-13. Sizes of the choices: `docs/notes/materials.md`.
- [x] **Review of 16 November 2026.** Independent check of the numbers, tests and documents. Every significance now states its detector threshold: the reference void is frozen above 0.2 and 1 GeV as well (11.52σ and 9.37σ at 1150 m against 12.05σ with every muon counted), 63 frozen cases; the scattering share is a mean over three seeds (0.74 ± 0.02 without a threshold, 0.92 ± 0.02 above 1 GeV) and has a validation test of the discrete mode; `docs/numbers.md` holds the headline numbers with their conditions. Found: Mulder applies decay along the air path; the cut-off survives under soil thinner than about 130 g/cm² (frozen as `thin_slab`); plain sums from a threshold are 2–3 % high. Materials refuse a repeated element and values outside the tables; the private-data guard allows a short list of file types instead of refusing a list; CI pins the backends and a weekly workflow meets their new releases; the pier of the spike is invented. Conventions: revision 3.
- [x] **0.1-05 Flux tables.** `muoscene.flux`. `FluxTable` gives the differential flux against kinetic energy, sine of elevation and opacity and its integral over a detector energy response (`EnergyResponse`, always given explicitly) by trapezoids that start at the threshold; it holds the open-sky flux at the detector altitude on a grid of energy and sine of elevation and takes the opacity through the CSDA range and stopping power of the reference material, which is what continuous transport does (conventions, revision 4). `MulderTable` builds it with Mulder — atmosphere model, optional date and azimuth for the geomagnetic field, reference chosen per conventions (GCCLY15 at sea level in 0.1.0; a tabulated reference is refused until 0.1-05b) — finds, stores and enforces the altitude cut-off, compares every new table with Mulder under a dense slab and caches it in the user cache directory by a hash of its inputs. `LegacyTable` repeats the arithmetic of the preliminary calculation to the last bit. Met: 60.50 m⁻² s⁻¹ sr⁻¹ above 1 GeV at sea level; the cut-offs of the spike and the frozen `slab` and `thin_slab` cases; within 0.045 % of Mulder under a slab at 16 150 random points up to 1 TeV, integrals above 0.2 and 1 GeV within 6·10⁻⁵; a site at −430 m has 56.69 m⁻² s⁻¹ sr⁻¹ and no cut-off; more than 3·10⁴ g/cm², energies below the cut-off and values outside the grid are refused; a sum that gives its first node a whole step is 2.9–3.3 % high by the vertical. Found: above 1 TeV the flux of Mulder under a thick slab wavers by 0.3 %; a table at the altitude of a detector under 10–30 m of soil is 0.05–0.10 % low, the air it has in place of that soil. 70 frozen cases. `docs/notes/flux.md`, `docs/numbers.md`.
- [ ] **0.1-05b Reference at the site altitude (0.1.1, after the release of 0.1.0).** Open-sky differential flux of muons in K and elevation for a given altitude, latitude and date from a model of the atmospheric flux: PARMA4 / EXPACS (T. Sato, PLoS ONE 10 (2015) e0144679 and 11 (2016) e0160390), which also gives electrons and positrons, with MCEq (optional `mceq` extra) as a cross-check above 1 GeV; tables cached and shipped for a grid of altitudes so that the core runs without either; passed to Mulder as a tabulated reference; the soft component as a table of its own for the track-density budget; normalisation against GCCLY15 at sea level; altitude dependence checked against published measurements at mountain altitudes (sources chosen in the task and listed in `docs/notes/reference.md`); a measured open-sky spectrum can replace the table. Whether PARMA holds at the zenith angles and energies needed, and where MCEq stops being valid at low energy, is established first. *Done when:* at sea level the table agrees with GCCLY15 within its stated uncertainty above 1 GeV; at 1150 m and 3340 m there is no cut-off and the flux above 1 GeV agrees with the sea-level reference carried up by Mulder, which already contains decay, within the size of the production effect and of the difference between the models, both quantified; the track density of open sky is given with and without the soft component; the choice of reference follows the conventions automatically.
- [ ] **0.1-06 Geometry.** Scene description: ground as an extruded cross-section, horizontal layers, box, cylinder, pile grid, niche, spherical anomaly; builder to a Calzone tree (centimetres, positions relative to mothers, subtraction of cuts, splitting of structures at layer boundaries, air at the site density); `Scene.scan` → `PathTable`; density hypotheses on the `PathTable`; checks (anomaly within one host, `Geometry.check` for overlaps). *Done when:* wall-with-niche path parity with the reference to 1e-9 m; analytic chords of sphere, cylinder and box; layers add up to the ground; at least 5e4 directions/s for the demo pier.
- [ ] **0.1-07 Site and detectors I.** `Site` (origin by latitude, longitude, altitude; ENU; CRS conversion in 0.2); `Pose` per conventions; `DirectionGrid` with exact bin solid angles and sub-sampling; `EmulsionStack` (area, films, cone, energy threshold, constant efficiency, projected area, track density above the threshold). *Done when:* grid solid angles sum to the analytic cone; pose round trips; legacy track-density parity within Monte Carlo tolerance; every output states its energy threshold; significances of targets seen through soil are given for 0.2 and 1 GeV until the response of the emulsion is known, while open sky below the cut-off waits for 0.1-05b.
- [ ] **0.1-08 Forward model.** Grid → scan → `PathTable` → equivalent opacity per hypothesis → table lookup → counts; `ExpectedMap`; the fast path is compared with direct Mulder flux on a random sample of directions in every run and the deviation is stored. The altitude at which the table of a detector under soil is taken is decided here: at the altitude of the detector the flux is 0.05–0.10 % low under 10–30 m of soil, at the altitude of the surface 0.03–0.05 % high (`docs/notes/flux.md`). *Done when:* deviation within 0.5 % on the demo; open sky and uniform slab match Mulder; a hypothesis without a new scan equals a rebuilt scene.
- [ ] **0.1-09 Significance and exposure.** Legacy cone estimator; binned χ² and Asimov likelihood-ratio significance; days to kσ; track density versus days against the scanning limit; Markdown table in the layout of the preliminary calculation. *Done when:* 10.3σ of the reference void reproduced in legacy mode within 2 %; unit tests of the formulas.
- [ ] **0.1-09b The preliminary scheme with the new physics.** The table of the preliminary scheme recomputed with altitude, CSDA thresholds, detector thresholds of 0.2 and 1 GeV and the scattering share from the discrete mode. Done in the private site workspace: nothing of it enters the repository except the method, shown on the synthetic canyon. *Done when:* each point of the scheme has its significance before and after, with the Monte Carlo error of the scattering share.
- [ ] **0.1-10 Examples.** `examples/canyon_demo` (generator: canyon depth and widths, two banks with different brows, two niche levels, a pier on each bank with a pile cap and 3 × 3 piles, two horizontal layers) with `site.yaml`, `detectors.yaml`, `run.yaml`; `examples/open_sky`, `examples/slab`. *Done when:* the parameters are documented and differ from any real site.
- [ ] **0.1-11 Configuration, pipeline, CLI, provenance.** pydantic schema for every 0.1 object (`extra="forbid"`, units in field names); loader with `include` and relative paths; `from_config` builders; orchestration in `muoscene.pipeline`; `muoscene check` and `muoscene plan`; run folder with resolved configuration, input hashes, package and backend versions, git commit, NetCDF and Markdown report. *Done when:* examples run from the command line; broken configurations give readable errors.
- [ ] **0.1-12 Visualisation I (0.1.1, after the release of 0.1.0).** Angular maps (counts, opacity, transmission, significance per hypothesis) in one style; PyVista scene (canyon, niches, pile caps, piles, anomalies, detectors with view cones coloured by significance) exported to HTML. *Done when:* the HTML opens in a browser and the scene matches the configuration.
- [ ] **0.1-13 Release 0.1.0.** README quickstart, `docs/legacy.md`, the scattering share of the demo void in discrete mode, next to that of the reference void (`tests/validation/test_scattering.py`), CHANGELOG, roadmap; CI green; tag `v0.1.0` (no Zenodo, no PyPI yet).

Order: 01 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 08 → 09 → 09b → 10 → 11 → 13, then 05b and 12 as 0.1.1 (changed on 16 November: at 1150 m the sea-level reference is valid for a threshold of 1 GeV in every direction from 15.7° of elevation up — the cut-off is 0.62 GeV at 25° and 0.98 GeV at 15.7°, and a table starts 10 MeV above it — and under more than a metre of soil for any threshold; the site-altitude reference is needed for open sky below 1 GeV, that is for the track-density budget and for a 0.2 GeV threshold in the open-sky half, and that needs the soft component too). Tasks 03–07 are independent of each other; 05b needs 05. Version 0.1 uses continuous transport only; scattering is quantified but not applied to maps.

## 0.2 — real sites

Coordinate systems with pyproj; true north, meridian convergence, magnetic declination from IGRF; mesh bodies (OBJ and STL with face normals) and DEM to a closed mesh through Calzone; survey import (LAS/LAZ, CSV points, LandXML, DXF, GeoTIFF) and point cloud to a closed mesh; layer horizons as nested height-field solids; structures crossing non-planar horizons; voxel overlay and ray-by-voxel path matrix; template of a private site workspace outside the repository.

## 0.3 — physics and planning

Seasonal atmospheres and seasonal site-altitude references; geomagnetic field as a systematic of the open-sky reference; mixed-mode tables for opacity beyond 3e4 g/cm²; scattering — discrete-mode maps for final numbers and a kernel calibrated on them for planning loops; angular efficiency tables; latent-image fading from temperature and humidity logs; track-density budget including transport and storage; systematics framework and profile-likelihood significance; sensitivity volume per voxel; optimisation of niches and tilts under film and scanning budgets; required accuracy of orientation and wall survey; report on how the full physics changes the preliminary numbers.

## 0.4 — data

`TrackSet` in `detectors.tracks`; synthetic tracks in the scanning output format; readers for scanning text output and FEDRA ROOT; binning with the same grid and cuts; data/model ratio, pulls, χ², region fits with nuisance parameters; straw telescope (level-1 response) and generic two-plane telescope; first comparison with an archival emulsion exposure.

## 1.0 — release and paper

Validation: analytic cases, the preliminary calculation, continuous against discrete transport, MUTE for intensities at depth, an archival exposure; documentation (guide, theory, configuration reference generated from the schema, API); one script per paper figure; Zenodo DOI; PyPI; software paper.

## 2.0 — inversion

Joint regularised inversion on the voxel grid with borehole priors; comparison with real exposure data.
